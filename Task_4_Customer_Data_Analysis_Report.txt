TASK 4: CUSTOMER DATA ANALYSIS
SHORT REPORT

Dataset: Random Customer Purchase Dataset
Records: 300 customers

1. OBJECTIVE
The objective is to understand customer purchasing behavior, segment customers based on age, location, and buying patterns, identify valuable customer groups, and suggest marketing strategies.

2. CUSTOMER PROFILE
- Average age: 34.8 years
- Average income: ₹57,599
- Average total spending: ₹11,940
- Average satisfaction: 7.03/10

3. CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION
Age groups:
- 18-25: 46 customers; average spend ₹15,857; average purchase frequency 6.65
- 26-35: 119 customers; average spend ₹10,769; average purchase frequency 5.40
- 36-45: 99 customers; average spend ₹11,452; average purchase frequency 6.23
- 46-65: 36 customers; average spend ₹12,147; average purchase frequency 6.14

Value segments:
- Low Value: 75 customers; average spend ₹4,310; average satisfaction 6.89/10
- Medium Value: 150 customers; average spend ₹10,650; average satisfaction 7.03/10
- High Value: 75 customers; average spend ₹22,148; average satisfaction 7.18/10

Engagement segments:
- Low Engagement: 38 customers; average spend ₹6,559; churn-risk rate 36.8%
- Medium Engagement: 187 customers; average spend ₹11,324; churn-risk rate 9.6%
- High Engagement: 75 customers; average spend ₹16,203; churn-risk rate 8.0%

4. LOCATION ANALYSIS
- Highest-spending location: Kolkata
- Average spending in Kolkata: ₹13,122
Location-level differences show that regional marketing can be useful.

5. PRODUCT CATEGORY ANALYSIS
- Highest-spending preferred category: Grocery
- Average spending for this category: ₹14,530

6. MOST VALUABLE CUSTOMER GROUPS
- Highest-spending age group: 18-25
- Highest-spending location: Kolkata
- Highest-spending category: Grocery
- Highest broad value segment: High Value

These groups should receive priority for loyalty programs, personalized offers, cross-selling, and premium customer experiences.

7. MARKETING STRATEGIES
- Give loyalty rewards and premium benefits to high-value customers.
- Use discounts and personalized recommendations to increase low customer engagement.
- Contact low-satisfaction customers with feedback and service-recovery campaigns.
- Use location-based campaigns, especially in Kolkata.
- Promote complementary products related to Grocery.
- Customize campaigns for different age groups.

8. CONCLUSION
Customer segmentation shows differences in spending, engagement, satisfaction, location, and product preference. Targeted marketing can improve customer retention, increase purchase frequency, and focus resources on the most valuable customer groups.
